How it works

Built to say only what it can prove.

1

Check

Messages with personal details are hidden and discarded. Requests for advice or returns get a polite facts-only reply.

2

Understand

It works out which scheme you mean and which single fact you're asking for, such as exit load or lock-in.

3

Retrieve

The fact comes from the scheme's official page or KIM. Other questions are answered by a search over 24 official pages.

4

Cite

Up to three sentences, exactly one source link, and the date the sources were last updated.

Privacy

There is no backend, no account and no analytics. Answers are computed in your browser from a pre-built index of official pages, and nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

Known limits

  • The facts are a snapshot from the date shown, not live data.
  • The Groww Multicap Fund page doesn't list an expense ratio or minimum SIP, so the app links the KIM instead of guessing.
  • It understands questions through keyword rules, so unusual wording can miss.
  • It answers about one scheme at a time, so each answer has one source.
